Trinetra

Truth clarity through smart design.

Trinetra is a misinformation detection startup concept designed to help users identify misleading content through clear visual indicators, educational context, and quick credibility checks. Built around the idea that truth should be accessible to everyone.

Current Focus

  • User-friendly interface for quick claim verification
  • Fact-check workflow and credibility scoring system
  • Research-backed UX flow for trust and clarity
  • Prototype design and feature prioritization

Planned Features

  • Claim analysis and breakdown dashboard
  • Source reliability scoring and insights
  • Content context and cross-reference support
  • Awareness modules for misinformation patterns

The Vision

In an era flooded with misinformation, Trinetra aims to be a calm, clear, and credible companion that helps users pause, verify, and understand before sharing. The product is designed with a focus on accessibility, simplicity, and trust — ensuring that anyone, regardless of technical literacy, can confidently navigate complex information landscapes. The name "Trinetra" refers to the third eye — representing insight, awareness, and clarity beyond the surface.
